OpenCiv2

Civilization II Multiplayer Gold Edition, rebuilt to run in your browser. It plays with your own copy of the game: the folder Civilization II is installed in (the one with Civ2Art.dll and RULES.TXT), the game's CD, or a disc image of the CD.
